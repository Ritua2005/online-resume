Digital Twin for Water-Quality Risk Prediction in Biofloc Aquaculture (2026)

Final-year project | Python, XGBoost, PyTorch, scikit-learn, Streamlit

  • Developed a software-only digital twin that predicts when a catfish biofloc pond needs a water change, using a real time-series sensor dataset of 222 records over about 74 days (25 positive events).
  • Cleaned and preprocessed the raw data, including mixed-unit temperature readings, and engineered features for classification.
  • Designed an ensemble of ANFIS fuzzy logic, a Bayesian classifier, XGBoost and a GRU network under a stacking meta-learner; added a Kalman filter for sensor state estimation.
  • Evaluated with stratified cross-validation and replicated two published IEEE methods on the same data; the fused model improved F1 from 0.05–0.21 to 0.56 (ROC-AUC 0.855).
  • Implemented a prescriptive layer that simulates a corrective action before recommending it, and built a Streamlit dashboard with risk trends and a what-if simulator.
